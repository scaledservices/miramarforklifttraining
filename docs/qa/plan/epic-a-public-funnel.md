# Epic A — Browsing the site like a new customer

**You are:** a person who just found Miramar on Google. **Not logged in.**
**Time:** ~20 minutes · [◂ Back to Start Here](README.md)

**Setup:** open a private/incognito window (so you're not logged in as anyone):
- Chrome: `Ctrl+Shift+N` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+N` (Mac)
- Safari: `Cmd+Shift+N`

---

## Story A1 — The homepage makes sense · P0

1. Click: **https://exquisite-perception-staging-725a.up.railway.app/**
2. Look at the whole page, top to bottom.

**You should see:**
- The Miramar logo and phone number (858) 901-0149
- Three ways to train, with **Onsite Training first**
- Onsite says **"Get a quote"** (NOT a dollar price)
- Hands-On Training shows **$280**
- Online course shows **$45**
- Photos look professional (new banners — tell me if any look off)

**Report if:** any price looks wrong, any image is broken, anything is in the
wrong language, or anything makes you go "huh?"

## Story A2 — Switching to Spanish · P0

1. On the homepage, find the language switch (EN/ES) in the top header.
2. Click **ES**.
3. Scroll the whole page again.

**You should see:** everything in Spanish — menu, buttons, prices, footer.

**Report if:** you see ANY English words left over (except the logo and
"Miramar"). Write down the exact English phrase and where it is.
